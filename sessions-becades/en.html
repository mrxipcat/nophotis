How to apply

Send an email to sessions@nophotis.com with the subject “Application – Signature Portrait – your name”.

Details to include

  • Full name, and stage name if you have one.
  • Contact phone number.
  • Date of birth (you must be 18 or over).
  • Town or city.
  • Instagram and, if you have one, website or online book.
  • Agency, if you have one.
  • Height and, if you wish, other measurements or features.
  • Experience: none, less than 1 year, 1–3 years or more than 3 years.
  • Why you are interested in this project, in a few lines.
  • Availability: weekday mornings, weekday afternoons or weekends.
  • How you heard about this call.
  • Whether you would like to hear about future calls.

Photos

  • 3 to 5 recent photos attached to the email, in JPG, PNG or WEBP, 5 MB maximum each.
  • At least one face shot and one full-body shot.
  • No filters or retouching.

By sending your application you confirm that you have read the sponsorship terms and the privacy policy.

Your photos and details are only used to assess your application and are deleted within 6 months if it is not selected.
