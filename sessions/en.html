Sponsored sessions – Personal projects

Each season I develop my own photographic projects and select a limited number of models to take part. The people selected receive a complete Signature editorial session, valued at 640 €, 100% sponsored.

It is not a made-to-measure session: we work on the concept of the current project, with pre-production, direction and retouching to the same standard as a paid session.
